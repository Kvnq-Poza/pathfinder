Why we built
PathFinder.

Navigating massive, deeply nested JSON responses shouldn't be a chore. We built PathFinder to make structured data exploration effortless, private, and fast.

The JSON Headache

As developers, we constantly deal with complex APIs that return large JSON objects. Finding the exact path for an optional chaining accessor or a JSONPath query often involves manual counting and guesswork.

PathFinder was designed to solve this by providing a high-performance visual tree that allows you to click any node and instantly get the access logic you need.

Privacy First

Your data is your business. Unlike many online formatters, PathFinder is 100% client-side. Your JSON never leaves your browser, and no server ever sees your data. It's zero-telemetry, zero-cloud tooling.
